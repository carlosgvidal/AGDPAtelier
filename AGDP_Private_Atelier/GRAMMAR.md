# Mineral composition grammar

Regimes: monolith, dyad, constellation, field, composite, sign.
Families: faceted, cabochon, slab/inlay, pearl.
Formal relations: continuity, counterpoint, friction.

The LoadGraph chooses the compositional regime and event coordinates. Those events alter the metal body before settings are built. Density and scale remain reciprocal: additional stones reduce individual scale. The interface is then restricted by lapidary family and material vulnerability.

A stone is valid only when its support, setting and exact seat are part of the accepted metal manifold.
