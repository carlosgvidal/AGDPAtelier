AGDP ATELIER — HIGH JEWELRY V4 / FOCAL MASS

Core change: gemstone planning now participates before metal morphology is finalized.
When a high-jewelry mineral program is active, competing metal nodes/rivets/screws are suppressed and one dominant mineral mass receives a semantic focal anchor.

Rules:
- FACETED: Asscher, cushion, emerald, princess, baguette only.
- Preferred settings: prong, invisible/invisible-window, inlay/inlay-window, channel-capture.
- Full bezel is not part of the V4 vocabulary.
- Brooch gemstone anchors are restricted to the front (+Z); posterior clip/mechanism is excluded.
- Ring/bangle/cuff anchors are exterior radial, never bore/body-contact anchors.
- Focal stones are intentionally large; no decorative satellite distribution in V4.
- Existing nodes/rivets/screws are suppressed when mineral focal mass is active, so metal does not compete with the gemstone.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
V4 changes morphology before metal generation and seats the gemstone proxy into a semantic focal anchor, but it does NOT yet boolean-cut a stone-specific seat or generate production prongs/channels/invisible-setting grooves in the metal manifold. Therefore the gemstone/metal interface is a design-stage structural proxy, not bench-ready setting geometry.
