AGDP ATELIER — HIGH JEWELRY V5

Structural change from V4:
- gemstone seat is now a boolean subtraction from the metal manifold before final audit;
- retaining metal is real geometry unioned into the manifold;
- PRONG = four structural prongs;
- CHANNEL-CAPTURE = two retaining rails;
- INLAY = recessed seat plus four low retaining rails;
- V4 pseudo-invisible is intentionally downgraded to channel-capture until a true girdle/rail invisible setting is modeled;
- the gemstone render/export reuses the exact resolved stone that drove the boolean operation.

Important limitation:
Existing holes/frames are still global morphology parameters, not explicit semantic boundary loops. V5 can preserve and favor that vocabulary, but it does NOT yet claim exact automatic filling of every pre-existing window. That requires exposing window boundary geometry from each typology builder.

Metal/gem optical materials are inherited from V4 and are not the focus of this revision.
