A GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT.

Structural objects for the body.

A GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT. develops contemporary objects in solid sterling silver .925 through a structural configuration system. The process does not begin with an inventory of finished jewelry. It begins with a typology, a finite field of operations and a decision made in real time. Each generated object is resolved as a singular arrangement of mass, interval, surface and connection.

The object is produced only after its configuration is complete. Computational precision defines the geometry; additive manufacturing translates it into metal; finishing restores the material presence of sterling silver. Every piece is therefore made to order rather than selected from stock. No generated structure is intentionally repeated, because repetition would cancel the condition that gives the object its identity: its emergence at the moment of execution.

This is not decoration understood as addition. It is structure brought into contact with the body. Rings, pendants, bracelets, earrings, cufflinks and other typologies operate as small architectures: objects that occupy space, register weight and expose the logic of their own construction.
