Structural Objects

Generated once. Produced to order.

A GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT. develops structural objects in solid sterling silver .925 through computational configuration. Each generated piece begins with a typology and a finite field of operations, then resolves as a singular arrangement of mass, interval, surface and connection.

Computational precision defines the geometry; additive manufacturing translates it into metal; finishing restores the material presence of sterling silver. Rings, pendants, bangles, cuffs, brooches, hoop earrings, cufflinks and ear cuffs are produced only after configuration. No generated structure is intentionally repeated.

The Atelier does not retrieve a predetermined model. It executes a configuration, produces a distinct object and preserves the relation between digital structure, metallurgical production and individual order.
