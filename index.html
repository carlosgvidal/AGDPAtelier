A GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT.

Jewelry as Material Evidence.

A GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT. is a contemporary jewelry practice working in polished sterling silver. Rings, pendants, bracelets, brooches, earrings, ear cuffs and cufflinks are generated through Atelier: a structural system that produces a new geometry at the moment of execution.

There is no fixed catalogue and no inventory of finished designs. A typology establishes the body of the piece; proportion, load, interval and surface determine its final form. The result is audited for production, translated into metal and assigned to a single order.

Each piece belongs to a coherent formal language, but no configuration is repeated. Its singularity is not imposed through a numbered edition after manufacture. It is established before the silver exists.

The work treats jewelry as architecture for the body and as material evidence of a decision. Computation defines the relationships. Sterling silver gives them weight, resistance and duration.
